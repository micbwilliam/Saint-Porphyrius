How the rules work (read me first)

1) Gender pairs (الشباب والبنات)

Seats are grouped in pairs per side of the aisle. In a 2|2 row: seats A·B are one pair, C·D the other. Within one pair everyone must be the same gender. A male pair and a female pair can share the same row across the aisle. (The real back row has 5 seats → its right side is a trio of 3 that must all match.)

2) Waiting list (قائمة الانتظار)

When every seat is taken, new people join the queue. When a seat frees (someone cancels) the engine walks the queue in order and gives the seat to the first person who is gender‑compatible with it. Others are skipped for this pass.
